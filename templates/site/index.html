Who we are

A registered service company you can rely on.

KF 4Fortken Holdings Ltd is a registered service company dedicated to providing professional cleaning, fumigation, garbage collection, landscaping, general supplies and allied facility management solutions.

Through our real estate arm we also offer property management, facilities management, rent collection, commercial leasing, land sales and valuation, so owners can look after their property through one trusted partner.

We operate with a commitment to quality, compliance and efficiency, ensuring that our clients receive reliable services aligned with the required standards and regulatory requirements.

Our Vision

To be the preferred provider of integrated environmental and facility support services in Kenya and the region, recognized for professionalism, sustainability and client satisfaction.

Our Mission

To deliver consistent, high-quality services that safeguard health, promote environmental responsibility and enhance operational efficiency for our clients, guided by integrity, accountability and trust.
